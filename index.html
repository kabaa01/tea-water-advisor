What your water is doing to the cup

    Terms used on this page

    pH
    Potential of Hydrogen — measures how acidic or alkaline water is, on a scale of 0 to 14.
    TDS
    Total Dissolved Solids — the amount of minerals dissolved in water.
    mg/L
    Milligrams per Litre — a unit used to measure the concentration of a substance in water.
    CaCO₃
    Calcium Carbonate — the reference compound used to express water hardness and alkalinity.
    SCA
    Specialty Coffee Association — the industry body that publishes coffee brewing-water standards.
    WHO
    World Health Organization.
    KEBS
    Kenya Bureau of Standards.

    Where these ranges come from

    Tea targets follow published brewing-water research on pH, TDS and catechin extraction. Coffee targets follow the Specialty Coffee Association's Golden Cup water standard. Fluoride guidance follows WHO/KEBS drinking-water limits. Full citations are in the project README. This tool estimates likely taste impact — it is not a certified water-safety test.
